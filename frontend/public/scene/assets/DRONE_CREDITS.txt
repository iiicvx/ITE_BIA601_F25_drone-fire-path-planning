Rescue Hexacopter (rescue_hexacopter.glb)
=========================================

Based on "Vampire UAV (BabaYaga)" by Karosio
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/vampire-uav-babayaga-f49e65781b32465c86841617e82fcae7
License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Changes made for this university project:
- removed the munition rack and the four bombs
- split the six propellers into separate nodes with pivots at the hubs (Prop_0..Prop_5)
- civilian rescue livery: red canopy, white body ring, "RESCUE" marking on top
- added navigation lights (red port, green starboard, white rear) and a top anti-collision strobe
- added one payload under the body: a fire-suppression capsule, released over the fire
- rescaled to real size (about 1.8 m across), textures converted to WebP, tangents generated

The modified model keeps the same license (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): non-commercial use,
credit the original author, and share any further modifications under the same license.
