Lunar color and elevation maps — NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio
CGI Moon Kit, visualization by Ernie Wright; LROC WAC / LOLA source data.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
lroc_color_2k.jpg — December 2025 color visualization, 2048 x 1024
ldem_3_8bit.jpg — LOLA elevation visualization, 1024 x 512
NASA reproduction guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The lunar asset is illustrative scenery. Its appearance does not represent an
astronomical ephemeris, and its elevation image is used only for subtle shading.

All assets are bundled locally: the running simulator makes no third-party
texture or environment requests. Flame density, smoke and grass movement are
authored visual effects and do not alter the mathematical planning/hazard model.
